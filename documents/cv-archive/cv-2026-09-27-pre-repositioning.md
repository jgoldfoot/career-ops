## Executive Summary

I design how AI systems behave with the people and agents they serve. In agent products, that behavior is the experience, which makes the behavioral layer the new UX. Not interface polish, but the systematic, testable specifications that govern how a system communicates confidence, handles uncertainty, and recovers when it gets things wrong. I lead the design, research, and content organizations that own this layer, and I have built the methodology that turns it into a buildable specification rather than a polish step at the end.

**Track record:** $11M ARR from a 0-to-1 AI-native product launch. A solo prototype I built in three weeks that closed a $1M annual contract. Design strategy behind Conviva's Visionary placement in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant. I wrote the book on this: How to Lead Design in the AI Era (2025). I also created BiModal Design, an open-source framework for interfaces that serve both human users and AI agents, and I contribute to OpenAI's Forum on applied AI+UX systems.

**Earlier career:** I founded two award-winning design firms that served clients such as Sony, Mattel, and Epic Software. That gave me a foundation in positioning, storytelling, and scalable design systems.
