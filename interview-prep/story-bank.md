# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### [AI Transformation] The AI-First Practice Transformation
**Source:** Report #001 — Ontra — VP of User Experience
**S:** Conviva's design-to-engineering handoff throttled shipping; frontend development was the bottleneck.
**T:** Transform how the design practice works, not just which tools it uses.
**A:** Moved the team off Figma onto an AI-first workflow. Built a custom design harness; PM and design co-author structured specs that feed Claude Code; a wiring agent connects to production.
**R:** Weekly releases with no frontend engineering in the pipeline. CPO: "Front-end development bottleneck disappeared overnight." One Builder produces what 5-8 traditional contributors produced.
**Reflection:** Adoption is org design, not tool rollout. The hard part was developing new judgment skills in designers, not installing software.
**Best for questions about:** AI adoption, change management, design ops, leading through disruption

### [AI Behavior] The Nexa Behavioral Contract
**Source:** Report #001 — Ontra — VP of User Experience
**S:** Nexa, Conviva's conversational analytics assistant, needed governed behavior: confidence, escalation, failure handling.
**T:** Make AI behavior specifiable and testable instead of vibes-based.
**A:** Authored a 72-clause Behavioral Contract: confidence thresholds, escalation triggers, trust recovery, failure states. Every clause testable by engineering, meaningful to users. Repeated with a 78-clause contract for the website sales agent.
**R:** Both shipped in production, proving in practice the thesis my book, *How to Lead Design in the AI Era*, had laid out. (Order matters: the book came FIRST as the thesis; the contracts are the production proof. Never say the contracts "became" the book's framework.) (Separately, I co-authored the peer-reviewed ACM CAIS 2026 paper on the Nexa insight-discovery system — DOI 10.1145/3786335.3813185 — which is about automated insight discovery, NOT the behavioral contracts. Keep the two distinct in interviews.)
**Reflection:** Behavioral judgment is the unowned layer in AI orgs. Writing it down as numbered clauses moved it from opinion to engineering requirement.
**Best for questions about:** AI product design, agentic experiences, trust and safety, designing for uncertainty

## Red-Flag Question Bank

- **"Your title is Senior Director; we want VP experience."** "I run a global design org at VP scope today: UX, research, and content across four continents, partnering directly with the CPO. The title catches up to the scope here."
- **"Can we review your portfolio?"** Outcome case studies plus live prototypes. Name the distinction: the role's frontier is behavioral judgment, not pixels; here is both.
- **"Why are you leaving Conviva?"** Exit narrative: built it, proved it, peer-reviewed it, now scaling it. Never negative on Conviva.
- **"You haven't worked in [domain]."** Contract-heavy enterprise workflow adjacency: Sepialine served legal-services clients for 8 years; Whip Media automated licensing contracts; Conviva is data-intensive intelligence UX.
